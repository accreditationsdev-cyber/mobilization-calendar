September 20 Cycle — Process POV

SEP 2 — ACCREDITATION SUBMISSION
Source: Qualified database from August 16–30 submission.

Purpose:
  • Submit accreditation database.
  • Establish the qualified population for the processing cycle.
SEP 5–9 — PARTIAL FILE CREATION
Responsible: CMU Specialist / Cost Team
  • Create partial file.
  • Prepare invoice compliance requirements.
  • Support Sales Team preparation.
  • Provide basis for Cost Management Supervisor funding.
SEP 10–11 — FUNDING ROUTING & HR TRANSMITTAL
SEP 10:
  • Submit to CMU Supervisor for funding routing.
  • Use file as basis for HR transmittal.
  • Prepare Wave 1 assessment from CMU Specialist Masterfile.
SEP 11:
  • CMU Supervisor submits to HR.
  • CMU Specialist releases partial Masterfile.
  • Sales receives Qualified / Not Qualified list.
SEP 12–13 — SALES ENDORSEMENT & INVOICE PREPARATION WINDOW
Sales Team:
  • Endorse concerns to CMU Specialist.
  • Request corrections.
  • Request adjustments.
  • Request additional sellers.
  • Request removal of sellers.
  • Prepare invoices for submission.
CMU Specialist:
  • Respond to Sales concerns.
  • Coordinate corrections and adjustments.
SEP 14–16 — INVOICE COMPLIANCE CHECKING
  • Submit invoices from qualified sellers.
  • Receive HR findings from the partial file.
  • CMU Specialist checks invoice submissions.
  • HR findings are integrated into invoice checking.
SEP 17–20 — FINAL HR VALIDATION
  • Sep 17 — Submit final file to HR with valid compliance invoices.
  • Sep 18–20 — HR conducts final wave of validation.
  • Sep 20 — CMU receives final additions/removals.
  • Sep 20 — Consolidated final list and findings are provided to Sales.
  • Sep 20 — Crediting Day.
